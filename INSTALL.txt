WEEK 9 UPDATE - VEHICLE RENTAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

1. Keep your existing C:\xampp\htdocs\VehicleRentalSystem folder.
2. Extract this ZIP.
3. Copy ALL files/folders inside VehicleRentalSystem_Week9_Complete into your existing VehicleRentalSystem folder.
4. When Windows asks, choose Replace the files in the destination.
5. This update DOES NOT contain or replace config/database.local.php, vendor, composer.phar, cookies.txt, or your .git folder.
6. Start Apache in XAMPP.
7. Open http://localhost/VehicleRentalSystem/
8. Test Register -> Login -> Book Vehicle -> My Bookings.
9. For Admin: in MongoDB Atlas users collection, change one test user's role from user to admin, log out, log in, then open /VehicleRentalSystem/admin/index.php.

IMPORTANT: Do not delete your existing config/database.local.php. It contains your MongoDB connection.
